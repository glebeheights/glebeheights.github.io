Membership Information

By virtue of your home ownership or residence here in the Glebe Heights community, you are eligible and encouraged to join our Community Association. Membership grants you access and use of the Beach / Picnic / Pier area, as well as boat ramp and slip access for additional fees.

Residing in the community does NOT automatically permit access to the community association property or beach areas (e.g. pier, tables, grill, porta potti, etc.). Membership in the association is required.

Contact information and a list of current family members must be provided. The Terms of Use Agreement below must be completed and signed and returned with this application.

GHCA Terms of Use Agreement

With the continued growth of our surrounding community and the Mayo peninsula in general, the Board has noticed a substantial increase in non-members trespassing upon the properties. This creates a substantial liability for the GHCA in regards to insurance and maintaining the quality of service and facilities offered by the GHCA. The following is a reminder of the terms of use outlined in the GHCA By-Laws and is provided here to encourage everyone to assist in keeping our facilities free of trespassers and violators of the established By-Laws. These are only an excerpt of the By-Laws; a full version of the GHCA By-Laws is available on the By-Laws page or by contacting the association at glebeheights.secretary@gmail.com.

ARTICLE X — USE OF ASSOCIATION PROPERTY

Section 1: Persons not members of the Association will be denied use of the beach, grills, firepit, piers and boating facilities, including boat ramp and any future improvements erected and maintained by authority of the Association.

Section 2: Each assessment-paying member shall be given one Identification Card. Guests must be accompanied to the beach, pier, boat ramp and facilities by a member, who shall be responsible for his guests' actions and conduct and, while being responsible, be held liable for costs payable for any damage to property or any item maintained by this Association.

Section 3: Each assessment-paying member shall be given one (1) key to the gate lock and one (1) key to the boat ramp lock depending on level of membership. Keys SHALL NOT be duplicated and SHALL NOT be given to any person other than the current members. Keys MUST NOT be given to guests under any circumstance.

ARTICLE XV — ADDENDUM

Section I: Added to these By-Laws are all rules incorporated in regards to the Community Beach operation. These rules shall be available for all members. Notice shall also be posted on the beach.

  1. Admittance to the beach by members and accompanied guests only.
  2. Admittance to pier by members and accompanied guests only.
  3. Boat ramp for use only by paid-up members of the Association ramp maintenance fee.
  4. Parking of cars for members only in designated areas. Members will be assigned two (2) Parking Passes per household and must display those passes in the front windshield of the vehicle when parked on the association property. Failure to display may result in the towing of the vehicle at the owner's expense.
  5. Open fires are ONLY allowed in the designated fire pit AND MUST be guarded by a member. All fires should be completely extinguished before leaving the property.
  6. No one allowed on the beach after 11 PM (unless due to circumstances beyond control).
  7. All picnic tables, etc. must always be neat and clean, with no trash of any kind to be tossed around.
  8. No animals allowed on the beach. Animals are allowed on other areas of the property but must be cleaned up after promptly.
  9. This is a private beach owned and maintained by the GHCA, Inc. Any defacing of property or damage of any means can and will be enforced by law.
  10. There will be no boat trailers left at the beach property for more than twenty-four (24) hours. Violators will be subject to towing at the owner's expense.
  11. Parking at the GHCA properties is for members and guests only using the given property. It is not an overflow parking area for private parties or events unless previously approved at a general meeting or by the Board.

By signing below, I agree to the terms of use and agree to abide by all of the terms provided in the GHCA By-Laws. A full copy of the By-Laws is posted for members on the By-Laws page.
